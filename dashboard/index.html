Barony — Fleet Dashboard

Git-native governance for teams of AI coding agents. Three visual treatments of the same live snapshot — projects, agents and their capabilities, fleet-health metrics, the merge queue, and the owner action queue.

Every number is read from the coordination repository by baron and committed as a curated, showcase-safe JSON file. No server, no database, no hand-entered figures — git is the source of truth and the whole page rebuilds from a clone.

How the data gets here

The coordination repository is private and stays private. dashboard/build-data.sh runs the read-only baron reporters against it and writes a one-way, sanitised projection to dashboard/data/fleet.json — the only thing published. Absolute paths, record bodies and handoff contents never leave.

Metrics are reported honestly: anything with an empty denominator renders as n/a with its basis rather than a flattering 0% or 100%, and single-observation figures are labelled n=1. Unmerged capabilities are shown as not active, not as passing.
